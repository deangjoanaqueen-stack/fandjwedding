Our Journey

College Days

They went to the same university, shared the same classrooms, and even appeared in the same class photos — yet never once spoke. Fritz was always the guy standing right behind Joana in every picture, like a quiet shadow fate refused to erase. Then life happened — they graduated, moved on, and forgot each other existed.

Fate Brings Us Back

Fast forward a few years: both single, both minding their own business. In 2023, Fritz tried to message Joana — just a friendly “hi.” Nothing. Seen-zoned. End of story. Or so he thought.

A New Year, A New Beginning

New Year’s Eve came around, and while Joana was busy eating twelve grapes under the table (yes, literally under the table 🤣), fate was busy planning a comeback. The next day, she posted something random on Instagram — and who else would reply but the same guy from those class photos: Fritz Bernard.

Yes to a Lifetime

From that small message, everything started to fall into place. One conversation led to another, and before they knew it, they were writing a story neither expected to begin. By the end of 2024, in the most unplanned moment ever, Fritz proposed. No drama. No script. Just two people who finally found each other — after years of standing in the same frame.
